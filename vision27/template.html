    How to play

    Follow the gold beams to finish Mama's list. Walk up to any car and take it. Click a board to see the advert, and click another player to follow them. When you both follow each other you are mutuals, and you can send head chat only mutuals see.

    Tap the mic button to talk to players near you. Tap the camera to show your face. Inside a building, everyone in the room can hear you. A green ring shows who is talking. Tap someone's bubble to mute them.

    Adverts

    These show on billboards, wall posters, bus stops and lamp banners around the city. Everyone sees your changes after you save.
